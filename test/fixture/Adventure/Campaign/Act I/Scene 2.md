# Scene 2 — The Market

Stalls line the square, and a [crow](<../../World/Monsters/Crow>) circles over them. A tinker sells a battered [Lantern](<../../World/Items/Lantern>) to anyone who asks, and asks nothing for it.

The lantern holds ${party.count{"wick", plus = 1, item = "World/Items/Lantern"}}: enough for one to burn tonight and one for every traveller after.
