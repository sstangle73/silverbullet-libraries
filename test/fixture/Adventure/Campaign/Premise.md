# Premise

A tin crown has gone missing from the river town of Ashford, and ${party.n()} strangers are the only people in town nobody suspects. ${party.N()} of them, one road out, and a warden who would rather the road stayed shut.

The adventure is written for a party of five and runs for three to seven characters.

## DM Only

The crown was never stolen. The Warden hid it, to have a reason to close the road.
