
# Premise

A tin crown has gone missing from the river town of Ashford, and five strangers are the only people in town nobody suspects. Five of them, one road out, and a warden who would rather the road stayed shut.

The adventure is written for a party of five and runs for three to seven characters.


\page


# Session Zero

Build the five characters together, out loud, and let each player say what their character wants from Ashford.

Everyone names one thing their character owes another, so that everybody owes somebody.


\page


# Act I

Ashford, its market, and the orchard beyond the ford. Three scenes, played in order.


\page


# Scene 1 — The Ford

Five travellers reach the ford at dusk. The water is knee deep and cold, and the far bank is where the town begins. A crow watches from each post.

Three clues wait on the bank, most important first: a bootprint, a torn ribbon, a coin. With fewer than three characters, one finds two; with more, two share one.

No roll to cross: the ford is slow, not dangerous.


\page


# Scene 2 — The Market

Stalls line the square, and a crow circles over them. A tinker sells a battered Lantern to anyone who asks, and asks nothing for it.

The lantern holds one more wick than the party has members, six for a party of five: enough for one to burn tonight and one for every traveller after.

*See Lantern: Rules.*

## The fight

**The old orchard.** A strangler and six creepers: a low-difficulty encounter for five level 1 characters (250 XP).

**The creatures.** Strangler — Monster Manual, *Blights* (vine blight). Creeper — Monster Manual, *Blights* (twig blight).

**Adjusting the Encounter.** For each character fewer than five, remove two creepers; for each one more, add two. Keep at least two creepers.

| Characters | Creepers | XP | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 | 150 | Low |
| 4 | 4 | 200 | Low |
| 5 | 6 | 250 | Low |
| 6 | 8 | 300 | Low |
| 7 | 10 | 350 | Low |

The strangler never moves; the creepers do.

# House Rules

## Checks

A check is rolled only when failing would change something. Where a roll can't change the outcome, the scene says what happens instead.

## Rests

A short rest takes an hour of quiet; a long rest needs a roof.


\page


# Travel

A day's travel is eight hours on foot. The ford can be crossed in daylight; at night it takes a check.

# The Warden

## First Impressions

A tall figure in a grey coat, with the keys of the ford on a chain.

## Who They Are

Ashford's warden keeps the ford and the road beyond it, and has for twenty years. Everyone in town has a story about the Warden's kindness.

## What They Want

The road closed for good, and the town grateful to whoever closed it.

## At the Table

Polite, tired, and never in a hurry. The Warden answers a question with a question.


\page


# Mara

## Who They Are

A ferrywoman who lost her ferry when the Warden closed the river crossing.

## What They Want

Her ferry back, or someone to blame for it.


\page


# Old Tam

## Who They Are

The Guild's oldest trader, who remembers the crown before it was tin.


\page


# The Tinker

## Who They Are

A peddler who sells in the market square and gives the lantern away.

## What They Want

To stay useful to the Warden, and out of the Warden's sight.


\page


# Wren

## Who They Are

A child from the far bank who knows every path through the orchard.

## What They Want

Someone to believe what she saw there.


\page


# The Guild

Ashford's traders, who need the road open more than anyone.


\page


# The Court

The Warden's household: clerks, gatekeepers and a handful of guards.


\page


# The Low Country

Flat, wet fields south of the ford, where the orchards are.


\page


# The High Country

Moor and stone north of the town, and the road the Warden wants shut.


\page


# Fordtown

The market town on the far bank of the ford.


\page


# Lantern

A battered tin lantern that burns without oil.

## Rules

- **Wicks.** The lantern holds a number of wicks, and the scene where it turns up says how many. Lighting it burns one.
- **Light.** A lit wick gives bright light in a twenty-foot circle for an hour.
- **Out.** Snuffing it early wastes the wick.

# Bestiary

The creatures of the adventure. Each names the published stat block to run it with.



## Strangler

A knot of briar the size of a man, rooted among the orchard's trees. One of the trees in any stand is awake, and which one is not obvious until something has hold of somebody.

### Run it as

Vine Blight — Monster Manual, *Blights*

**Rooted.** It never moves and never follows.

#### Where it turns up

- Act I, Scene 3: one at the middle of the old orchard



## Creeper

A bundle of dry twigs that pulls itself along the ground.

### Run it as

Twig Blight — Monster Manual, *Blights*

#### Where it turns up

- Act I, Scene 3: half-buried around the orchard's clearing



## Crow

The Warden's crows watch the road and fly home at dusk.

### Run it as

Raven — Monster Manual, *Animals*

#### Where it turns up

- Act I, Scene 1: on the ford's posts
- Act I, Scene 2: over the market
